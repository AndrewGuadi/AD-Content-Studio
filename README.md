## Editorial guardrails
Published, planned and proposed items are distinct. Content is human-led and fact-checked. Neither AI system should publish, contact prospects or expose client data without authorization.

**Privacy notice:** Repository visibility was public at last check. Keep secrets, customer material and private production data out until access controls are reviewed.
