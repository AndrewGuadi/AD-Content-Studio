# Authentic Dynamics — editorial position

## Audience
Small-business owners in Central Pennsylvania, extending to Lehigh Valley and anywhere content is useful. Not every business needs a complicated website or AI system.

## Company
Authentic Dynamics LLC builds websites, connected systems, practical growth technology, automation and experiments.

## Core positioning
- **We build. You own.**
- **Better digital. Stronger local.**
- **No mystery in the middle.**
- We build businesses online; we build community offline.
- A website is an owned business asset, not just decoration.
- Social generates discovery; a website helps create clarity, trust and action.
- Explain platform tradeoffs, ownership, recurring fees, maintenance and portability plainly.
- Avoid scare tactics, exaggerated technical claims, and unexplained SEO/AI spending.

## Voice
First-person, face-to-camera, direct, curious, human and locally grounded. Technical but non-patronizing. Not scripted agency copy or generic AI motivation. Preserve casual warmth and actual speech rather than synthetic presenters.

## Preferred package per video
Title, specific viewer problem, hook, 30–60s natural script, shot/recording plan, factual caveats, overlay text, edit notes, cover, caption, CTA, roughly five relevant hashtags, series/episode tag, next-episode bridge and source notes.

## Visual notes from earlier planning
Proposed brand visual references: dark #111111, warm ivory #F5F5F0, lime #B8FF63; strong typography, genuine Central PA/business photography, understated transitions. These are historical creative suggestions, not a locked design-system specification.

## Guardrails
No made-up client outcomes; verify external statistics; protect customer data, credentials and private links; don't suggest security or AI tools are infallible. Teach before selling. Prioritize genuine camera presence; support with graphics where useful.
