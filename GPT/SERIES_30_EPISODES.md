# Three 30-episode series — planned editorial calendar

**Status:** Proposed from Oct 2026 GPT planning. These are not all published or scripted. “Day” means curriculum position, not a forced daily publishing commitment.

**Recommended launch:** Monday AI for PA Businesses; Wednesday The Internet; Friday Build in Public. 90 planned topics at three weekly releases would provide about 30 weeks of programming. Maintain existing Digital, Explained and static/brand content separately. Each reel should stand on its own. Aim 30–60 seconds (some technical demos may go to 90s), face-to-camera with purposeful supporting visuals.

## Editorial rules

1. Each episode answers one question and offers an observable outcome or concrete example.
2. Do not fabricate demonstrations, results, customer reviews, dates, audience engagement, or security guarantees.
3. Keep AI, coding, privacy and legal caveats accurate and proportionate; source time-sensitive facts before shooting.
4. Identify published records by link and date, not solely by inconsistent numbering.
5. Every full episode package should include hook, spoken script, shot list, on-screen text, assets, cover, caption, five hashtags, CTA, sources and a next-episode bridge.
