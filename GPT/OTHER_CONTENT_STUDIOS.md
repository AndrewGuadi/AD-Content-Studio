# Earlier Authentic Dynamics planning — other content studios

These are historical outlines and topic banks, not claims of publication.

## Digital, Explained
Founder-led educational videos (normally around 60 seconds) explaining small-business technology. Recurrent themes: website ownership and portability, domain names and hosting, usability, SEO and local search, business profiles, automated workflows, websites as business assets. See PREVIOUS_CONTENT_AND_STATUS.md for known recorded titles.

## Static content program
Non-video single images and carousels: original observations, myths and realities, definitions, checklists, questions, mini-infographics, useful red flags, and short comparisons. Community-oriented, practical, locally grounded; no pushy promotion.

## Website breakdowns
Practical analyses of websites, platforms and prices. Suggested formats included "What Would This Website Cost?", "What I Would Fix First", "Platform Breakdown", "One Business, Three Website Options", "Where the Money Actually Goes", "What Do You Actually Own?", and "The Story Behind the Website". Compare suitability, initial and recurring cost, developer fee versus platform fee, portability, maintenance, technical burden and accessibility. No invented client stories.

## Authentic Dynamics founder/community storytelling
Real-life stories of client work (with permission), community activity, practical engineering decisions, lessons learned and experiments. Story arc: goal, difficulty, discovery, change and result. Not every post should sell a service. Protect identities and private information.

## Build-in-public creative experiments
Earlier proposed recurring frames: "Can I Build This?", "I Had an Idea", "Let's See If This Works", "Weekend Build", "Built at Authentic Dynamics", and "Digital Experiments". Topic proposals included QR tools, invoice and PDF conversion, EXIF inspection/removal, business calculators, browser utilities, document Q&A and multiplayer games. Some are demonstrations of existing applications; others are ideas.

## AI for PA Businesses
Practical use cases: voice notes into emails, Google review drafts, document summaries, customer FAQs, better service descriptions, notes into checklists, business option comparisons, interview questions, extracting information from PDFs and images, identifying administrative repetition, and data privacy.

## The Internet
Video-first learning track for beginners, moving through networks, browser/web/server concepts, code, operating systems, security and eventually practical systems programming. Founder should appear on camera, with accurate, minimal graphics.

## Content standards
For every major piece, produce: episode angle, audience relevance, hook, complete script or carousel, recording plan, on-screen text, verification notes, caption, CTA, hashtags, cover title and next idea. Distinguish previously recorded from proposed. Long-form exact scripts from earlier chats are not yet fully recovered verbatim.
