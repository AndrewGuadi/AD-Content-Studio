# Muse production handoff — Episode 001 of each series

**Status:** Proposed request; Muse connection and capabilities must be independently confirmed.

## Intent
Muse should serve as an efficient creative researcher, editor and production assistant, not an autonomous brand impersonator or content farm. Preserve the founder's actual face and voice. Review original Instagram reels and transcripts, connected GitHub projects, approved brand assets, Hugging Face tools and available skills. Do not claim access to tools or material that hasn't been verified.

## Three launches

1. **AI for PA Businesses — 001: What Is This Series?** Suggested tag `#AIForPABusinesses`. Make practical AI understandable for Pennsylvania owners. Start with a genuine example (rough notes to professional email). Central promise: AI needn't be complicated to be useful.
2. **The Internet — 001: What Is This Series?** Suggested tag `#TheInternetExplained`. Begin a series from how networked computers communicate toward servers, code and cybersecurity. Tease 002: What Actually Is the Internet?
3. **Build in Public — 001: What Is This Series?** Suggested tag `#BuiltAtAuthenticDynamics`. Introduce prototypes, utilities, apps, successes, errors, code and curiosity. Ground in real existing projects, not inventions.

## Deliverables per launch
- Audience and concise learning promise; strongest hook.
- Natural spoken script 30–60 seconds, face-to-camera by default.
- Storyboard, camera shots, needed screen captures and restrained graphics.
- 9:16 Instagram cover and on-screen text with reasonable UI safe space.
- Full caption, suggested hashtags (not pseudo-Instagram tags), one useful CTA, subtitles and accessibility notes.
- Source check and rights/privacy review; clear evidence of what exists versus a proposed prototype.
- List actual available assets, assets to create and what the owner must record.
- Connection to Episode 002.

## Work process
Research and plan from accessible sources; report gaps; produce editable assets; submit for founder approval. Do NOT publish/schedule, modify unrelated repositories, access private customer material, recreate likeness/voice synthetically, or send outreach without explicit permission. The repository is public at last check; do not upload sensitive material.

## Outreach philosophy
Teach in public, show real demos and respond to real questions. Do not generate fake personal engagement, bulk unsolicited pitches, fabricated testimonials or false claims. High-quality useful examples build recognition and are preferable to automated mass messaging.
