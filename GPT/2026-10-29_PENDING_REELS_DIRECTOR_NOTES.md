## A-002 — AI for PA Businesses: Start With One Job
**Single viewer promise:** A local business owner can try one low-risk, repeatable task with AI, not automate the whole business.

Six planned beats / suggested overlay concepts:
1. **0–5s hook (founder face):** "You don't need to automate your entire business to get started with AI." Avoid generic claim that AI always saves money.
2. **5–15s pain point:** Owners repeatedly respond to customer inquiries. Show a short, fictional inbox/notes mockup.
3. **15–35s specific demo:** Fictional deck quote inquiry -> short prompt asking for address, photos, preferred date -> professional draft reply. Show clear before/after on screen, not only abstract animated blocks.
4. **35–45s human judgment:** "Review it, adjust it, then decide what to send." Do not suggest auto-sending without validation.
5. **45–60s next step:** "Pick one task you repeat every week." Give one concrete starter activity.
6. **End:** One calm, identifiable Authentic Dynamics signoff. Do not imply the example is a real customer's message.

**Editorial issue to test:** A full 80.4 seconds is acceptable only if the viewer actually gets to see the demo and can read the text. If most time is stills+voice, tighten pauses or simplify.

## B-003 — The Internet: What Your Wi-Fi Is Doing All Day
**Single viewer promise:** Having a working local Wi-Fi link does not guarantee an internet connection.

Six planned beats:
1. **0–5s hook:** "You can have a strong Wi-Fi signal and still have no internet."
2. **5–15s distinction:** Wi-Fi is radio-based local networking; the internet is a wider network of networks.
3. **15–35s accurate diagram:** Phone -> wireless access point (often built into home router) -> local router / modem or ONT -> internet service provider -> wider internet. Simplify as needed but avoid falsely teaching the router alone equals the internet.
4. **35–50s real case:** ISP link fails; phone still associates with Wi-Fi, can potentially reach local devices (depending on network), while websites fail to load.
5. **50–70s takeaway:** local connectivity vs wider connectivity. Label the broken link distinctly rather than crossing out the Wi-Fi icon.
6. **70–80s wrap:** one memorable line, gentle segue to 'what happens when you load a website.'

**Accuracy QC:** Separate Wi-Fi from home Internet service; router and access point can be separate devices; saying 'Wi-Fi connects devices to the internet' is incomplete. Check that every on-screen arrow/line remains consistent. No fake error messages presented as genuine screenshots.
