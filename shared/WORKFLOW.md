# Collaboration & editorial workflow

## Division of labor
**GPT:** Research synthesis, source-aware scripting, curriculum structure, factual review, strategic planning, review of Muse proposals.
**Muse:** Where authorized and connected, gather account analytics, original transcript data, suggest media treatments, create approved production assets and render drafts.
**Human owner:** Source of truth for lived experience, client approvals, appearance/voice, editorial sign-off, outreach, publishing and final go/no-go.

## Episode stages
`idea → researched → scripted → fact-checked → recorded → edited → approved → scheduled → published → measured`
Never mark a stage completed without evidence.

## Metadata schema
`series | episode | title | audience | hook | learning goal | script | shot list | b-roll | cover | caption | hashtags | verified sources | rights | CTA | status | original URL | metrics/date`.

## Rules
- Keep real voice and face; no synthetic impersonation without express approval.
- Avoid AI hype, unsupported statistics, synthetic testimonials, misleading scarcity or invented results.
- Every video stands alone; favor plain English and real demonstrations.
- Use neutral examples or explicit client permission.
- Never expose accounts, API keys, private repos, customer records, website vulnerabilities or infrastructure details.
- Label proposals versus recorded/published content; avoid assuming an idea has shipped.
- Keep source/claim verification adjacent to scripts.
- Instagram tags are proposed organization, not evidence of active Instagram series.
- GitHub access for GPT does not establish Muse's access.
